# Interview Transcripts

**Task:** DIP-4 · **Related:** [`relevance-evidence.md`](relevance-evidence.md)

Transcripts of five user interviews about choosing furniture and planning room layouts. Each interview was an audio recording in English that followed the same short question list (a condensed version of the guide in `relevance-evidence.md` §3). Recordings were received on 23 October 2026.

**How these were produced.** The audio was transcribed automatically with Whisper (`large-v3-turbo`). The text below is Whisper's output; the only edits are punctuation, capitalisation and splitting into **Q** (interviewer) and **A** (participant) turns. Grammar is not corrected. Words the model most likely misheard are marked `[sic: likely …]`. Timestamps `[m:ss]` refer to the start of each turn in the recording.

**Limitations.** Whisper tends to drop fillers ("um", "uh"), false starts and repeated words, so this is close to, but not strictly, verbatim. Marked uncertainties should be checked against the audio.

**Privacy and consent record.** The interviewer introduced the sessions as voluntary and said responses would be anonymous. The supplied transcripts preserve an explicit agreement to anonymous notes for P3; individual yes/no consent confirmations are not recorded for P1, P2, P4, or P5. Participants are identified here as P1–P5, and names and audio files are not stored in the repository. See the participant and record audit in [relevance-evidence.md](relevance-evidence.md).

## Question list

The interviewer opened each session with (minor wording varies): *"Hello, we are conducting a university project about choosing furniture and planning room layouts. Your participation is voluntary and you can skip any question or stop at any time. We will keep your responses anonymous."*

1. Tell me about the last time you bought furniture. Where did you look and what influenced your choice?
2. How do you check whether furniture will fit in your room? Have you ever experienced a problem with furniture not fitting?
3. How do you decide where to place furniture in a room?
4. Have you ever used an app or website to plan a room or visualize furniture? If yes, what did you like or dislike? If not, how do you plan instead?
5. What is the most difficult or time-consuming part of choosing furniture or arranging a room?
6. Would you use a tool that recommends furniture arrangements based on your room dimensions, style and budget? What features would be most important to you?
7. What would make you trust the tool's recommendations? Would you prefer using it on a computer or a mobile phone?
8. Is there anything else you would like to share about furniture shopping or room planning?
